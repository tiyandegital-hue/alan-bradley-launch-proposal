Services

Exactly what's included.
No fine print.

Each pillar lists what you get and what you don't. If it's not on the list, ask — we'd rather say so now than surprise you later.

01

Website build

A multipage website designed around your company — not a template with your name pasted in. You approve a live preview before anything goes public.

Included

  • Home, About, Services & Contact pages
  • Copy written in plain language
  • Mobile-first, fast loading
  • Contact forms that reach your inbox
  • Basic search-engine setup

Not included

  • Online store / payments (separate project)
  • Logo design (we can recommend someone)
  • Content you don't provide (text, photos)
02

Hosting & domain setup

The technical foundation — set up once, correctly, in your name. You never touch a control panel unless you want to.

Included

  • Domain search & registration in your name
  • Hosting configured & connected
  • SSL certificate (the padlock)
  • Automatic backups

Not included

  • Ongoing hosting fees (paid to the host, at cost)
  • Domain renewal (yearly, at cost)
03

Business email

Professional addresses on your own domain — configured properly so clients see you, not their spam folder.

Included

  • Addresses like you@yourcompany.com
  • Set up on your phone & laptop
  • SPF / DKIM authentication
  • Simple guide for adding addresses later

Not included

  • Mailbox subscription fees (at cost, if any)
  • Migrating years of old email archives
